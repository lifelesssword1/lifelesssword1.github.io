Fair Slice: Cutting Chef – Privacy Policy

Developer: SGD Studios · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Fair Slice: Cutting Chef ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by SGD Studios ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (level, coins, stars, purchases you unlocked in the game, and settings such as language and sound) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or choose "Reset progress" in Settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address (used to estimate your general location, such as the country),
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • information about your interaction with ads, and diagnostic and crash information.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, a consent message from Google lets you accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • All Android users: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can turn off your internet connection while playing; the game works offline, but ads and some rewards may not be available.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalized advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized advertising and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. In-app purchases

If the game offers purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google using the links above. You can also contact us at any time.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
